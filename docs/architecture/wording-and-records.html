Wording that outruns its record

A pass over every sentence the product shows an owner turned up three of the same mistake, and none of them were sloppy prose. Each was a line that described something no record establishes: a page that collects nothing promising collected output, a page arguing that only a restore proves a backup promising a restore you can trust, and a stage reporting a failed attempt when the only thing that failed was a check on a schedule. The rule they all break is one rule.
